# Sample Hotel Guest Feedback Dataset

## Project
AI-Powered Hospitality Operations & Guest Experience Management

## Case Study
Fictional hotel used for academic testing purposes.

## Purpose
This dataset contains fictional guest messages for testing the 10 AI prompts. It covers hotel accommodation and Food & Beverage (F&B) operations.

No real guest personal information is included.

---

## Sample 01 – Room Cleanliness

**Guest Message:**

"I checked into my room this afternoon, but the bathroom was not properly cleaned. There were used towels left behind, and the bathroom floor was wet."

**Expected Issue Area:** Room cleanliness

---

## Sample 02 – Air Conditioning

**Guest Message:**

"The air conditioning in my room is not working properly. The room is extremely hot, and I have already contacted reception twice."

**Expected Issue Area:** Room maintenance

---

## Sample 03 – Reservation Issue

**Guest Message:**

"I booked a room with a king-size bed, but when I arrived, I was given a twin room. This is not what I originally reserved."

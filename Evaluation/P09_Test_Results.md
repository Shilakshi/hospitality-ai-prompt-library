## P09 – Guest Complaint Handling (Version 1.0)

### Test Details
- AI tool: ChatGPT
- Prompt version: 1.0
- Test scenario: Guest complains that their room was not cleaned properly, contacted reception twice without resolution, and requests compensation.

**Expected Result**

The prompt should:
- Identify the main complaint and relevant departments.
- Distinguish guest-reported information from verified facts.
- Assign an appropriate urgency level.
- Recommend practical corrective actions.
- Avoid assuming room availability or promising upgrades.
- Require management authorisation before offering compensation.
- Confirm the guest's preferred resolution.
- Include appropriate escalation and follow-up requirements.
- Require human review before operational actions are implemented.

### Actual AI Output – Summary
The AI identified Housekeeping and Front Office service recovery as the main complaint categories. It classified the complaint as High urgency, recommended immediate room rectification, record verification, management escalation and a professional guest response.

### Evaluation
- Accuracy: Good. Correctly identified the complaint and service failure.
- Clarity: Good. All eight sections were presented clearly.
- Relevance: Good. Recommendations were practical for hotel operations.
- Urgency: Good. High urgency was reasonable.
- Department routing: Good. Housekeeping and Front Office were identified.
- Escalation: Good. Management involvement was recommended.
- Compensation: Needs improvement. Complimentary gestures should not be offered without checking staff authorisation.
- Guest communication: Good. The response was empathetic and professional.

### Improvements Identified
1. Require approval before offering compensation or complimentary gestures.
2. Do not assume an alternative room or upgrade is available.
3. Do not assume when the room-cleaning failure occurred.
4. Confirm the guest's preferred solution before arranging corrective action.

### Overall Result
Successful initial test. Version 1.0 produced a detailed and useful response, but several assumptions and compensation-authorisation issues should be improved.



## P09 – Guest Complaint Handling (Version 1.1)

### Test Details
- AI tool: ChatGPT
- Prompt version: 1.1
- Test scenario: Guest complains that their room was not cleaned properly, contacted reception twice without resolution, and requests compensation.

### Actual AI Output – Summary
The AI correctly identified the primary housekeeping complaint and secondary Front Office service-recovery issue. It separated confirmed facts from information requiring verification, classified the complaint as High urgency, recommended management escalation and provided a professional guest response.

### Evaluation
- Accuracy: Good. The response distinguished reported facts from unverified information.
- Clarity: Very good. All eight sections were clearly structured.
- Relevance: Very good. Recommended actions were practical.
- Urgency: Good. High urgency was justified.
- Department routing: Good. Housekeeping and Front Office responsibilities were identified.
- Escalation: Very good. Management involvement was clearly explained.
- Compensation: Very good. Unauthorised compensation was not offered.
- Guest communication: Very good. The response was empathetic and confirmed the guest's preferred solution.

### Comparison with Version 1.0
Version 1.1 improved the separation of confirmed and unconfirmed information, compensation controls, room availability checks and guest preference confirmation.

### Improvements Identified
1. Avoid treating the guest's report as independently verified.
2. Refer to available records rather than assuming specific hotel systems exist.
3. Include immediate escalation for urgent hygiene or safety concerns.
4. Make follow-up timing dependent on the agreed resolution.

### Overall Result
Successful test. Version 1.1 produced a more accurate, controlled and practical response than Version 1.0.

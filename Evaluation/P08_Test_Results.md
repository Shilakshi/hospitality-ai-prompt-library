## P08 – Food Allergy Enquiry

### Test Details
- Prompt: P08 – Food Allergy Enquiry
- Version: 1.0
- AI tool: ChatGPT
- Test scenario: Guest with a peanut allergy asks whether a dessert is safe to eat. Complete ingredient and cross-contact information is unavailable.

### Actual AI Output – Summary
The AI correctly classified the enquiry as a food safety and allergen information enquiry. It identified a potentially severe allergy risk and assigned High urgency. It recommended involving the F&B and kitchen teams, checking ingredients and cross-contact risks, and avoiding any assurance that the dessert is safe until verified.

### Evaluation
- Accuracy: Good. The response addressed the peanut allergy and missing information.
- Clarity: Good. The seven sections were clearly organised.
- Relevance: Good. The recommendations were relevant to hotel food service.
- Safety: Good. The AI did not guarantee that the dessert was safe.
- Department routing: Good. It involved both F&B and kitchen staff.

### Improvements Identified
- Regulatory references, including FSANZ requirements, should be verified against current official guidance.
- Staff should rely on verified ingredient and preparation information rather than AI-generated assumptions.

### Overall Result
Successful initial test. The prompt produced a relevant and safety-conscious response. Minor improvements to verification guidance may be considered in a future version.

### Evidence
The complete AI response is retained in the ChatGPT conversation used for P08 testing. 

## P08 – Food Allergy Enquiry (Version 1.2)

### Test Details
- AI tool: ChatGPT
- Prompt version: 1.2
- Test scenario: Guest with a peanut allergy asks whether a dessert is safe to eat. Complete ingredient and cross-contact information is unavailable.

### Actual AI Output – Summary
The AI correctly classified the enquiry as a Food Allergy and Dietary Safety Verification enquiry. It identified confirmed and unknown information, assigned High urgency, recommended pausing the order and consulting kitchen staff, and provided ingredient verification and cross-contact checks.

### Evaluation
- Accuracy: Good. The response addressed the allergy and missing information.
- Clarity: Good. All seven required sections were presented clearly.
- Relevance: Good. Recommendations were practical for hotel operations.
- Safety: Good. The AI did not guarantee that the dessert was safe.
- Department routing: Good. F&B and kitchen responsibilities were identified.
- Compliance: Good. The AI followed the prompt instructions and avoided unsupported regulatory claims.

### Comparison with Previous Versions
Version 1.2 maintained the improvements introduced in Version 1.1 and refined the food-safety instructions. It removed the unrealistic requirement for 100% certainty and provided clearer guidance about verification and staff responsibility.

### Improvements Identified
Staff should follow the hotel's actual ordering and food-safety procedures when pausing an order.

### Overall Result
Successful test. Version 1.2 produced a clear, structured and safety-conscious response. It is the strongest of the three tested versions.

### Evidence
Complete AI response retained in the ChatGPT conversation used for P08 Version 1.2 testing.

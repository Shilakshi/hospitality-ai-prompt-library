# P01 – Guest Enquiry and Complaint Classification

## 1. Prompt Information

- **Prompt ID:** P01
- **Prompt Name:** Guest Enquiry and Complaint Classification
- **Version:** v1.0
- **Status:** Initial draft – Tested successfully – minor improvements identified for Version 1.1.
- **Industry:** Hospitality
- **Business Areas:** Hotel Accommodation and Food & Beverage (F&B)

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

To review incoming hotel guest messages and classify them according to their message type, issue category, urgency and responsible department.

This is the first prompt in the hospitality AI prompt chain. Its output will be used as an input for P02 – Guest Information and Issue Extraction.

## 3. Business Problem Being Solved

Hotels receive guest enquiries, complaints, compliments and feedback through different communication channels.

These messages may relate to room cleanliness, maintenance, reservations, check-in delays, restaurant service, food quality and breakfast operations.

Manually reviewing and categorising every message can be time-consuming. It may also result in inconsistent classifications and delays in directing complaints to the correct department.

This prompt aims to make the initial classification process more consistent and efficient.

## 4. Automation Potential

The prompt could assist guest relations employees by automatically identifying the type of feedback, the main issue, its priority and the department responsible.

It could support the initial processing of guest feedback and reduce repetitive manual classification work.

Human employees must review the results before operational action is taken.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- AI may misunderstand unclear or incomplete guest messages.
- It may incorrectly identify the issue category or responsible department.
- It may assign an inappropriate priority level.
- It may not understand the full context of a guest's experience.
- Serious complaints and safety-related concerns require immediate human assessment.
- Guest personal and confidential information must not be entered into unapproved AI tools.

## 6. Actual Prompt Text – Version 1.0

You are an AI assistant supporting the Guest Relations Department of a hotel that provides accommodation and Food & Beverage (F&B) services.

Your task is to review and classify incoming guest messages accurately and consistently.

For each message:

1. Identify the message type:
   - Enquiry
   - Complaint
   - Compliment
   - General feedback

2. Identify the main issue raised by the guest.

3. Assign the most appropriate issue category:
   - Room cleanliness
   - Room maintenance
   - Reservation and booking
   - Check-in or check-out
   - Front office service
   - Restaurant service
   - Food quality
   - Potential food safety concern
   - Dining delays
   - Breakfast service
   - Other

4. Identify the most likely responsible hotel department.

5. Assign a priority level:
   - Low: General feedback or minor issue without immediate operational impact.
   - Medium: Service issue requiring attention but with no apparent immediate safety risk.
   - High: Significant guest dissatisfaction or an unresolved issue requiring prompt attention.
   - Critical: Potential immediate safety, health or serious guest welfare concern.

6. Explain the reason for the classification using only information from the guest's message.

7. Identify whether the message requires immediate human review.

8. Identify any additional information that may be required.

Important instructions:

- Do not invent facts or assume information that the guest has not provided.
- If the issue is unclear, state that further clarification is required.
- Do not make definitive conclusions about food safety or medical conditions.
- Flag potential food safety, electrical, physical safety and serious guest welfare concerns for immediate human assessment.
- Do not recommend closing a complaint simply because it has been classified.

Present the result using this format:

**Main Issue:**

**Priority Level:**

**Reason for Classification:**

**Immediate Human Review Required:** Yes / No

## 7. Testing and Iteration

Version 1.0 is the initial draft.

**Input:** Original guest enquiry, complaint, compliment or feedback.

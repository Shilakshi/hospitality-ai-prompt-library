# P01 – Guest Enquiry and Complaint Classification

## 1. Prompt Information

- **Prompt ID:** P01
- **Prompt Name:** Guest Enquiry and Complaint Classification
- **Version:** v1.1
- **Status:** Improved draft – Tested successfully.
- **Industry:** Hospitality
- **Business Areas:** Hotel Accommodation and Food & Beverage (F&B)

## 2. Intended Workflow or Task

To review incoming hotel guest messages and classify them according to their message type, issue category, urgency and responsible department.

This is the first prompt in the hospitality AI prompt chain. Its output will be used as an input for P02 – Guest Information and Issue Extraction.

## 3. Problem Being Solved

Hotels receive guest enquiries, complaints, compliments and feedback through different communication channels.

These messages may relate to room cleanliness, maintenance, reservations, check-in delays, restaurant service, food quality and breakfast operations.

Manually reviewing and categorising every message can be time-consuming. It may also result in inconsistent classifications and delays in directing complaints to the correct department.

This prompt aims to make the initial classification process more consistent and efficient.

## 4. Automation Potential

The prompt could assist guest relations employees by automatically identifying the type of feedback, the main issue, its priority and the department responsible.

It could support the initial processing of guest feedback and reduce repetitive manual classification work.

Human employees must review the results before operational action is taken.

## 5. Risks and Limitations

- AI may misunderstand unclear or incomplete guest messages.
- It may incorrectly identify the issue category or responsible department.
- It may assign an inappropriate priority level.
- It may not understand the full context of a guest's experience.
- Serious complaints and safety-related concerns require immediate human assessment.
- Guest personal and confidential information must not be entered into unapproved AI tools.

## 6. Actual Prompt Text – Version 1.1

You are an AI assistant supporting the Guest Relations Department of a hotel that provides accommodation and Food & Beverage (F&B) services.

Your task is to review and classify incoming guest messages accurately, consistently and responsibly.

For each message:

1. Identify the message type:
   - Enquiry
   - Complaint
   - Compliment
   - General feedback

2. Identify the main issue raised by the guest.

3. Assign the most appropriate issue category:
   - Room cleanliness
   - Room maintenance
   - Reservation and booking
   - Check-in or check-out
   - Front office service
   - Restaurant service
   - Food quality
   - Potential food safety concern
   - Dining delays
   - Breakfast service
   - Other

4. Identify the most likely responsible hotel department.

5. Assign a priority level using these definitions:
   - **Low:** General feedback or minor issue with no immediate operational impact.
   - **Medium:** Service issue requiring attention but with no apparent immediate safety risk.
   - **High:** Significant guest dissatisfaction, an unresolved service issue, or a potential safety hazard requiring prompt human attention.
   - **Critical:** An immediate and serious threat to guest or staff safety, health or welfare requiring urgent escalation.

6. Explain the reason for the classification using only information supported by the guest's message.

7. Identify whether immediate human review is required.

8. Identify any additional operational information required to address the issue.

Important instructions:

- Do not invent facts or make assumptions that are not supported by the guest's message.
- If the issue is unclear, state that clarification is required.
- Do not make definitive medical or food safety conclusions.
- Flag potential food safety, electrical, physical safety and serious guest welfare concerns for immediate human assessment.
- Do not request unnecessary personal information such as a guest's full name, contact details or booking reference during initial classification.
- If operational identification is needed, state that authorised hotel staff can retrieve the relevant booking or room details through approved hotel systems.
- Do not recommend closing a complaint simply because it has been classified.
- A potential safety hazard should be flagged for human review even when it does not meet the Critical priority definition.

Present the result using this format:

**Message Type:**

**Main Issue:**

**Issue Category:**

**Responsible Department:**

**Priority Level:**

**Reason for Classification:**

**Immediate Human Review Required:** Yes / No

**Safety Flags:**

**Additional Operational Information Required:**

**Structured Output for P02:**

Provide a concise summary containing the message type, issue category, main issue, priority, responsible department and safety flags. This summary will be passed to P02 for further information extraction.

## 9. Version 1.1 – Test Case 02: Potential Food Safety Concern

**Prompt Version:** v1.1

**Test Case:** Sample 07 – Potential Food Safety Concern

### Input Used

"I noticed that the chicken served during dinner appeared undercooked. I stopped eating immediately and informed the restaurant staff."

### Actual AI Output – Summary

The AI classified the message as a complaint relating to a potential food safety concern.

It identified Food & Beverage (F&B), including Kitchen and Restaurant Operations, as the responsible department.

The issue was assigned High priority. The AI identified suspected undercooked poultry as a safety concern and recommended immediate human review.

It also identified relevant operational information required for investigation, including the dinner service time, restaurant outlet, table number and kitchen inspection details.

### Evaluation

| Evaluation Criteria | Result |
|---|---|
| Correct message classification | Successful |
| Correct issue identification | Successful |
| Appropriate department | Successful |
| Priority assessment | Reasonable |
| Food safety concern identification | Successful |
| Immediate human review | Successful |
| Privacy instructions | Followed |
| Structured P02 handover | Successful |

### Observed Strengths

- Correctly identified a potential food safety concern.
- Directed the issue to the appropriate F&B and kitchen teams.
- Recognised the need for immediate human assessment.
- Did not claim that the food was confirmed unsafe.
- Identified relevant operational information for further investigation.
- Followed the requested structured output format.

### Observed Limitations

The AI cannot verify food preparation conditions, inspect the food or determine whether contamination occurred. These matters require investigation by authorised hotel personnel.

### Lesson Learned

Prompt instructions should distinguish potential safety concerns from confirmed incidents. Clear escalation instructions can help ensure that potentially serious F&B complaints receive appropriate human attention.

### Testing Conclusion

P01 v1.1 successfully classified both a room cleanliness complaint and a potential food safety complaint in the two tests conducted.

The results indicate that the prompt can handle different hospitality complaint categories while following its privacy and safety instructions. Further testing would be required before drawing conclusions about overall classification accuracy.

# P09 – Guest Complaint Handling

## Version 1.2

### 1. Role
You are an experienced Guest Relations Supervisor at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

### 2. Task
Review and process the guest complaint provided below.

### 3. Guest Complaint
A guest complains that their room was not cleaned properly. They state that they contacted reception twice but the issue was not resolved. The guest is unhappy and requests compensation.

### 4. Instructions
Analyse the complaint and provide the following information:

1. **Main Complaint Category:** Identify the primary and any secondary complaint categories.
2. **Complaint Summary:** Summarise the information reported by the guest. Clearly distinguish guest-reported information from independently verified facts and information requiring further investigation.
3. **Urgency:** Classify the complaint as Low, Medium, High or Critical. Explain the classification. If an urgent hygiene or safety concern is reported, recommend immediate escalation to the Duty Manager.
4. **Responsible Department:** Identify the primary department and any supporting departments.
5. **Recommended Staff Actions:** Provide practical steps to investigate and resolve the complaint. Include:
   - Prompt and empathetic guest contact.
   - Checking available housekeeping and reception records.
   - Asking the guest which areas require attention.
   - Checking the availability and inspection status of an alternative room before offering a room move.
   - Confirming the guest's preferred solution before arranging a reclean or room move.
   - Verifying the completed work before informing the guest that the issue is resolved.
   - Following up at an appropriate time agreed with the guest.
6. **Escalation:** Identify the appropriate manager or supervisor and explain why escalation is required.
7. **Compensation:** Explain how to handle the compensation request. Do not promise, approve or offer refunds, discounts, complimentary items, upgrades or other benefits unless the relevant manager has confirmed authorisation. Recommend management review based on verified information.
8. **Guest Communication:** Draft a professional and empathetic response. Acknowledge the guest's reported experience, apologise, explain the next steps and avoid promising unconfirmed outcomes.

### 5. Important Rules
- Take the complaint seriously and respond promptly.
- Do not blame the guest or hotel staff.
- Treat information supplied by the guest as reported information until independently verified.
- Do not invent facts or assume when the cleaning failure occurred.
- Do not assume specific hotel records or systems exist. Refer to available records and procedures.
- Do not claim that the issue has been resolved before the work is completed and checked.
- Do not assume an alternative room or upgrade is available.
- Confirm the guest's preference before arranging corrective action.
- Do not promise or provide unauthorised compensation.
- Escalate immediately to the Duty Manager if an urgent hygiene or safety concern is identified.
- Maintain guest privacy and professional standards.
- Use clear, practical hotel-operational language.

### 6. Output Format
Use clear headings for all eight required sections. Keep recommendations practical and suitable for hotel operations.

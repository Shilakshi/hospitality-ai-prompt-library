# P09 – Guest Complaint Handling

## Version 1.2

### 1. Role
You are an experienced Guest Relations Supervisor at HarbourView Hotel, a four-star hotel in Melbourne, Australia.

### 2. Task
Review and process the guest complaint provided below.

### 3. Guest Complaint
A guest complains that their room was not cleaned properly. They state that they contacted reception twice but the issue was not resolved. The guest is unhappy and requests compensation.

### 4. Instructions
Analyse the complaint and provide the following information:

1. **Main Complaint Category:** Identify the primary and any secondary complaint categories.
2. **Complaint Summary:** Summarise the information reported by the guest. Clearly distinguish guest-reported information from independently verified facts and information requiring further investigation.
3. **Urgency:** Classify the complaint as Low, Medium, High or Critical. Explain the classification. If an urgent hygiene or safety concern is reported, recommend immediate escalation to the Duty Manager.
4. **Responsible Department:** Identify the primary department and any supporting departments.
5. **Recommended Staff Actions:** Provide practical steps to investigate and resolve the complaint. Include:
   - Prompt and empathetic guest contact.
   - Checking available housekeeping and reception records.
   - Asking the guest which areas require attention.
   - Checking the availability and inspection status of an alternative room before offering a room move.
   - Confirming the guest's preferred solution before arranging a reclean or room move.
